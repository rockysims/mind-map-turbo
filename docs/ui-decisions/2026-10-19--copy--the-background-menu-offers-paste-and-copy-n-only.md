# The background menu offers "Paste" and "Copy N" only

- Situation: the menu that opens on a hold or right-click on empty canvas, once a node selection exists.
- Proposed: "Paste here", "Select all" and, while a selection exists, "Clear selection".
- Chosen: two items at most — "Paste" (the point is implied by where the menu opened), and "Copy N" when more than one node is selected, where N is the count. "Select all" and "Clear selection" are not in the menu; Cmd/Ctrl+A, a click on empty canvas and Escape keep those jobs.
- Why: no reason given ("Paste here" → "Paste"; remove "Select all" and "Clear selection"; add "Copy [number]" when the number of selected nodes is greater than 1).
